# Nominee Form App

Fills the Great Eastern **Appointment / Change of Nominee(s)** form (NBZ-FNOMI-V07-082025) from:

- the policyowner's PDF (ePartner Online Enquiry printout, e-Policy, proposal or an earlier nominee form), and
- the customer's WhatsApp reply (pasted text, a .txt / chat export, or a photo / screenshot).

It checks every detail against the form's rules (NRIC, shares = 100 %, relationship vs gender, Muslim / trust-policy notes…), shows a page-by-page preview, and downloads the filled PDF.

**Privacy:** everything runs in your browser. Customer details are never uploaded. The saved customer list lives only in the browser on your own device. Photo reading (OCR) downloads the text-reading engine from jsDelivr on first use; the photo itself stays on your device.
